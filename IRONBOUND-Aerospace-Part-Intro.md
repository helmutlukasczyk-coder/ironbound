### Beyond the Ground Battle

Aerospace support reaches beyond the lance's line of sight. Interceptors contest the approach, strike fighters threaten exposed targets, and a well-timed attack can open the way for the ground force.

This archive collects the approved fighter configurations used in IRONBOUND. Each record provides movement, weapons, ammunition, heat, protection, and campaign values.

**IN THIS PART**

Light fighters; medium fighters; heavy fighters.

Records are arranged alphabetically by design. Use the printed configuration and the main rulebook's aerospace procedures.
