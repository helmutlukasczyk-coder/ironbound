### Steel Beneath the Giants

Across the Inner Sphere, armored crews fight beneath the shadows of BattleMechs. Scout hovercraft sweep the flanks, tanks secure the roads, and missile carriers bring firepower to positions the enemy thought were safe.

This archive collects sixteen approved ground vehicle configurations for IRONBOUND. Each record provides the vehicle's movement, weapons, ammunition, armor, structure, and campaign values.

**IN THIS PART**

Tracked tanks; wheeled assault guns; hovercraft; missile and direct-fire carriers.

Records are arranged alphabetically by vehicle. Use the printed configuration and the Ground Vehicle Combat rules in the main rulebook.
