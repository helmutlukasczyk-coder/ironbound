### The Ground They Hold

Between the machines of war, infantry crews secure streets, guard approaches, and occupy the positions a lance has fought to reach. Their strength lies in cover, coordination, and knowing when to move.

This archive collects six conventional infantry profiles for IRONBOUND. Each record represents one platoon and supplies its movement, weapons, casualty tracks, and campaign values.

**IN THIS PART**

Rifle foot platoons; machine gun foot platoons; laser foot platoons; SRM foot platoons; rifle jump platoons; SRM jump platoons.

These are IRONBOUND house profiles. Use them with the Infantry procedures in the main rulebook.
