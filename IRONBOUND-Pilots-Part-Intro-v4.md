### The People Behind the Machines

A company is built around the people who answer its call. New recruits bring ambition, veterans bring hard-earned judgment, and every MechWarrior brings a story that can become part of the campaign.

This directory collects 144 original IRONBOUND pilots across four experience levels. Each entry provides an identity, individual skills, campaign specialties, and personal hooks, with starting talents and Signatures where listed.

**IN THIS PART**

Rookies; Regulars; Veterans; Elites.
